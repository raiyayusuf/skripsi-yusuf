==================================================
METODE PENELITIAN
==================================================

R&D = metode penelitian (payung)
ADDIE = model pengembangan (langkah)

Tahap ADDIE:
1. Analysis       - analisis kebutuhan, observasi GSE, analisis kurikulum
2. Design         - rancang hardware, modul, labsheet, instrumen
3. Development    - bikin produk, uji fungsionalitas, validasi ahli, revisi
4. Implementation - uji coba ke siswa GSE, ambil data respons
5. Evaluation     - analisis data, revisi akhir

Model lain (referensi): 4D, Borg & Gall, Dick & Carey, Lee & Owens.
Keputusan: ADDIE (Branch, 2009).
